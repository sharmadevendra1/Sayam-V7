5. Data Deletion - How to Delete Your Account & Data

Google Play Data Deletion Requirement - You can request deletion anytime:

Method 1: In-App (Fastest - 1 click)

  1. Open SAYAM App → Profile / Settings → Delete Account
  2. Confirm "Yes, Delete my account and data"
  3. Your account, name, email, RIASEC answers, reports deleted within 7 days

Method 2: Email Request

  1. Email us at info@sayamconsulting.com
  2. Subject: Delete My Account - [your registered email]
  3. Body: Please delete my SAYAM account and all associated data
  4. We process within 7 days and send confirmation email

What gets deleted: Name, Email, Phone, RIASEC answers, Reports, App activity
What is retained: Purchase invoices retained for 90 days for legal/tax compliance, then auto-deleted. No data retained beyond 90 days after deletion request.

Partial Data Deletion (Without Deleting Account)

To delete only specific data (e.g., only test history but keep account): Email info@sayamconsulting.com with what you want deleted. We support partial deletion on request.

6. Data Retention
